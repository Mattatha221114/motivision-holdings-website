About MOTIvision Holdings

A shared vision, built into a business

MOTIvision began in 2019 as a shared vision between two people, Masikhwa Onkonisa and Tshamano Israel. The business was formally registered in 2021.

MOTIvision was originally established as an income-generating business, with the intention of helping support and fund Live4U Foundation. As the business developed, a practical need became clear: many small business owners needed help understanding company registration, compliance requirements, documentation, administration and procurement processes.

MOTIvision therefore expanded its services to provide practical business support, and later evolved into MOTIvision Holdings as the wider group developed.
