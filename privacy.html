Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Information We Collect

When you contact MOTIvision Holdings through this website, WhatsApp, phone or email, we may collect information you provide directly, such as your name, business name, phone number, email address, service required and message content.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to respond to enquiries, prepare quotes, deliver requested services, and communicate with you about your enquiry or ongoing service.

  • To respond to enquiries submitted through this website, WhatsApp, phone or email
  • To prepare service quotes and proposals
  • To deliver the services you have requested
  • To maintain records related to services provided

Contact Form

The enquiry form on this website is not currently connected to an automated email delivery system. We recommend confirming any enquiry submitted through the form by also contacting us via WhatsApp, phone or email.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with relevant third parties, such as government departments or authorities, only where necessary to deliver the specific service you have requested (for example, a registration or compliance submission).

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information shared with us, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you by contacting us using the details on our Contact page.

Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact MOTIvision Holdings at info@motivision.co.za or 064 784 5167.
