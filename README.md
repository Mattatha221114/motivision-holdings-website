# About MOTIvision Holdings

MOTIvision Holdings provides practical business support and consulting services to businesses, entrepreneurs and organisations in South Africa.

Our services include:

- Business registration and compliance
- Tender and procurement administration
- Business documentation
- Funding and business development support
- Digital solutions
- Website development and maintenance
- Branding and business identity support

MOTIvision focuses on practical administrative and business support designed around the needs of growing businesses.

---

# Our Origin

MOTIvision began in **2019** as a shared vision between **Masikhwa Onkonisa** and **Tshamano Israel**.

The name **MOTIvision** combines:

- **MO** — Masikhwa Onkonisa
- **TI** — Tshamano Israel

The original vision was to establish an income-generating business that could also support and contribute towards the work of **Live4U Foundation**.

As the founders worked with small businesses and entrepreneurs, they identified recurring challenges involving business registrations, compliance, documentation, administration and access to procurement opportunities.

MOTIvision subsequently developed its service offering around these practical business needs.

The business was formally registered in **2021** as **MOTIvision Enterprises** and later evolved into **MOTIvision Holdings** as the organisation developed a broader group structure.
